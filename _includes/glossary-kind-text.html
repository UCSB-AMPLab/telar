{%- comment -%} A kind's panel label (`part="label"`) or glossary-page heading (`part="heading"`), assigned to `glossary_kind_text`. A site kind carries its own, written in the site's language; a core kind names a language key, read from `lang`, the site's catalogue. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if include.part == "label" -%} {%- assign glossary_kind_text = include.kind.label -%} {%- assign glossary_kind_key = include.kind.panel_label -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign glossary_kind_text = include.kind.heading -%} {%- assign glossary_kind_key = include.kind.section_heading -%} {%- endif -%} {%- unless glossary_kind_text -%} {%- assign glossary_kind_path = glossary_kind_key | split: "." -%} {%- assign glossary_kind_text = include.lang[glossary_kind_path.first][glossary_kind_path.last] -%} {%- endunless -%}
