{%- comment -%} The glossary entries of one kind, assigned to `glossary_kind_entries`. Pass the kind (see glossary-kinds.html) as `kind`. The build writes a kind id into every entry's page as `glossary_kind`; an entry without one is of the default kind, so no entry is left out of every kind. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign glossary_kind_id = include.kind.id -%} {%- if include.kind.default -%} {%- assign glossary_kind_entries = site.glossary | where_exp: "entry", "entry.glossary_kind == glossary_kind_id or entry.glossary_kind == nil" -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign glossary_kind_entries = site.glossary | where: "glossary_kind", glossary_kind_id -%} {%- endif -%}
